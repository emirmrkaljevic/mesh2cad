Convert STL to STEP with real surfaces

Most "STL to STEP" converters wrap every triangle in a STEP face. The file opens in CAD but you cannot edit it: thousands of faces, no cylinders, no holes to resize. mesh2cad fits the mesh with planes, cylinders, cones and chamfers and writes an exact B-rep STEP.

Steps

  1. pip install mesh2cad (Python 3.10 or newer)
  2. mesh2cad part.stl -o part.step
  3. Open part.step in FreeCAD, Fusion 360, SolidWorks or Onshape.

When it works and when it refuses

  • Works best on mechanical parts made of planes, cylinders, cones and chamfers (brackets, holders, printer parts).
  • Organic shapes (figurines, scans, sculpted surfaces) are not recovered as exact surfaces.
  • Every export passes a self check against the original mesh (deviation and volume error). If a part fails, mesh2cad refuses and tells you why instead of giving you a wrong model.
  • On our 6 Prusa printer test parts: exact STEP for 6 of 6, editable CadQuery/OpenSCAD for 5 of 6 (v0.14.0).

mesh2cad is free and open source (see the GitHub repository for the license). Found an STL it handles badly? Open an issue with the file.
