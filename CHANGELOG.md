# Changelog

## 0.26.0 - 2026-11-11

- Parametric CadQuery, OpenSCAD, and FreeCAD export group plain Z through-holes when they form a regular pattern. A row of three or more holes, one diameter, collinear centers, and one pitch, is `hole_row_1_n`, `hole_row_1_pitch`, `hole_row_1_d`, the start `hole_row_1_x` / `hole_row_1_y`, and `hole_row_1_angle` (degrees, 0 along +X). An axis-aligned rectangle of at least two by two is `hole_grid_1_nx`, `hole_grid_1_ny`, `hole_grid_1_pitch_x`, `hole_grid_1_pitch_y`, the corner, and `hole_grid_1_angle`. The same names with a `base_hole_` or `leg_hole_` prefix are used for the foot and the upright of a filleted L-bracket. A fully filleted box uses the same `hole_row_*` / `hole_grid_*` parameters, cut after `edge_fillet_r`. Holes that do not fit the pattern stay `hole_N_*`. `mesh2cad edit`, `mesh2cad params`, and the MCP tools `list_params` / `edit_params` list and edit the new numbers, including the count. A count has to be a whole number from 1 to 200. An edit that puts a hole outside the wall is refused and nothing is written.
- Two holes are not a row. One gap is not a pattern. A bolt circle is not detected. Blind holes, countersinks, counterbores, and holes along X or Y stay individual parameters. Extrusions and combined booleans are not grouped. A center has to lie within max(4·tol, 0.25) mm of the ideal pitch. If the patterned solid misses the usual gate (max deviation at most max(4·tol, 0.1), volume error at most 1%), the script is written with one parameter set per hole.
- On a 100×40×6 mm plate with four Ø6 holes at x = -18, -6, 6, 18 (pitch 12), CadQuery and OpenSCAD write `hole_row_1_n = 4`, pitch 12, diameter 6, start (-18, 0), angle 0. With no noise the limit is 0.100 mm. CadQuery deviates by 0.0037 mm (volume error 0.004%) and OpenSCAD by 0.0037 mm (0.003%). At 0.05 mm the limit is 0.200 mm and all 3 seeds stay a row of 4 with pitch 12.0 and diameter 6.0. The worst is seed 0: CadQuery 0.1683 mm (volume error 0.010%), OpenSCAD 0.1713 mm (0.009%).
- A 100×70×6 mm plate with a 3 by 2 grid of Ø5 holes (pitch 20 along X, 24 along Y, corner at (-20, -12)) is `hole_grid_1_nx = 3`, `hole_grid_1_ny = 2`. With no noise CadQuery deviates by 0.0031 mm (volume error 0.002%) and OpenSCAD by 0.0031 mm (0.002%). At 0.05 mm the limit is 0.200 mm and all 3 seeds stay that grid. The worst is seed 0: CadQuery 0.1705 mm (volume error 0.014%), OpenSCAD 0.1705 mm (0.013%).
- Four collinear holes with gaps 12, 14, and 10 mm stay `hole_1` through `hole_4`. A row whose last center is 0.6 mm past an equal pitch is detected, then refused by the deviation gate, and written as four individual holes. `mesh2cad edit plate.py --set hole_row_1_n=5` on the four-hole plate renders one valid solid with five holes. The volume drops by one cylinder, π·3²·6 mm³.
- A 90×40×16 mm box with every edge filleted and four Ø6 holes at pitch 16 is `hole_row_1_n = 4` after `edge_fillet_r`. At tol 0.05 mm CadQuery deviates by 0.0235 mm (volume error 0.000%) and OpenSCAD by 0.0220 mm (0.000%). The filleted L (outer 40×30 mm, legs 8 mm, height 12 mm, r=3) with three Ø4 foot holes at x = 16, 24, 32 and y = 4 is `base_hole_row_1_n = 3`, pitch 8. With no noise CadQuery deviates by 0.0036 mm (volume error 0.003%) and OpenSCAD by 0.0032 mm (0.002%).
- FreeCAD writes the macro when the same CadQuery check passes. `freecadcmd` was not on PATH for this measurement, so the FreeCAD deviation is not reported. The macro text uses the same pattern parameters. The command still says the FreeCAD solid itself was unverified.

## 0.17.0 - 2026-11-01

- `mesh2cad params script.py` lists the named parameters of a CadQuery, OpenSCAD, or FreeCAD script mesh2cad generated: name, value, kind, and unit when one is known (`base_x`, `hole_1_d`, `hole_1_depth`, `fillet_r`, `extrude_len`, `outline`, and the rest). `mesh2cad edit script.py --set hole_1_d=8 --set base_x=+2` rewrites those numbers and leaves every other byte unchanged. A leading `+` or `-` is a delta. It re-renders with CadQuery in-process, or with `openscad` / `freecadcmd` when that program is on `PATH`, and prints volume and bounding box before and after, and whether the solid is valid. `--in-place` or `--output-script` writes the script. `-o` writes STEP or STL. OpenSCAD writes STL. If the program is missing, the script can still be rewritten and the command says it did not render. `-o` in that case writes nothing.
- An unknown parameter is refused and the message lists the valid names. An edit that is not one valid solid, or a hole larger than the wall it sits in, is refused and no file is written. Outline point lists are listed and are not edited as a single number.
- MCP tools `list_params(script_path)` and `edit_params(script_path, changes, output_path?)`. `edit_params` updates the script in place and returns the old and new values, the rendered path, validity, the volume and bounding-box delta, and warnings.
