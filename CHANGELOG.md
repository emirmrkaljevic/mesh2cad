# Changelog

## 0.14.1 - 2026-10-28

- Fix: the CLI failed to import on Python 3.10 and 3.11 with a SyntaxError, because one f-string reused double quotes inside the braces (allowed only from Python 3.12). The package declares Python 3.10 or newer, so `pip install mesh2cad` gave a broken command on those versions. CI now byte-compiles the sources on Python 3.10 as well.

## 0.14.0 - 2026-10-27

- Parametric export rebuilds a combined extrusion after the silhouette intersection and the partial-depth union have both missed the mesh. Each outline is a raster snapped to vertical wall planes. A hole that has broken out through a slot is closed at the two mouths, and a flare outside that cylinder is left on the loop. A planar bevel with axis-aligned walls is a triangular cut (`cut_N`) along the wide part of the face. A 45 degree cone that is not already `hole_N_chamfer` is `cone_N_r0`, `cone_N_r1`, `cone_N_h`. Shallow floors are scored again on that solid. The rebuild does not run when an earlier solid already passed the gate. The gate is unchanged.
- y-rod-holder exports both scripts. CadQuery and OpenSCAD deviate by 0.1300 mm (volume error 0.096% and 0.089%), under the limit of 0.138 mm. On 0.13.0 the same part was refused at 0.745 mm and 0.61% volume: the Z outline followed the bore through the slot and chorded the slanted -Y wall. The Ø3.3 hole along Y at x=-11.65, z=-5 keeps the Ø6.3 counterbore. plug-aligner (0.0000 mm), endstop-block (0.0421 mm), y-belt-idler (CadQuery 0.1534 mm, OpenSCAD 0.1639 mm), and y-motor-holder (0.1843 mm) are unchanged. Exact STEP is unchanged on all six parts.
- y-belt-holder stays refused: "combined extrusion deviates by 2.517 mm (limit 0.154), volume error 1.97%". The belt profile is not a wall-snapped extrusion.
- Tests: a 2 mm 45 degree chamfer on the middle of one edge of a block, for both exporters. The square ends keep the silhouettes rectangular, and the chamfer is `cut_1`. A plate with a slanted wall and a slot into a hole rebuilds an outline that keeps the slanted corner and meets the hole at two mouths.
- Parametric export recovers a counterbore whose wall is only part of a turn. A ring of planar facets becomes one cylinder when the facets sit on a principal axis, the arc is a single span from 100 to 340 degrees, the bore inside the arc is empty, and the cylinder is not a fillet tangent to two planes. A flat polygon whose corners lie on a circle stays planes, because the face centres do not. The cylinder is used only when it is coaxial with a through hole and larger than that hole, and only when the stack is then a counterbored through hole. Otherwise the hole stays the one the full cylinders already described. Segmentation does not call this fit, so STEP faces are unchanged.
- When that counterbore stops short of the bounding end because a tab elsewhere sets the end, and the gap from the end to the mouth is already empty, the depth is measured from the part end out to the shoulder. A gap that still contains material is left alone.
- Tests: a block with a through hole and a counterbore that breaks out through a side face, for both exporters, including a tab that sets the part end past the mouth. Editing the counterbore diameter changes the volume by the part of the annulus that lies inside the block. A 90 degree fillet and a flat ring of chords are not returned as cylinders.

## 0.13.0 - 2026-10-25
- Parametric export can now write a union of partial-depth extrusions when the intersection of full-length silhouettes misses the mesh. Each extrusion has its own outline and length. A second outline on the same axis is `extrude_z2_len` / `outline_z2` (the first of that axis is `extrude_z1_len`; one extrusion per axis keeps the old names). A clip on one extrusion is `extrude_z2_clip1_x` (and y, z, d) and cuts only that extrusion. A 45 degree chamfer on the start face of a Z extrusion is `extrude_z1_chamfer` and `extrude_z1_chamfer_at`. A hole that opens on an intermediate face carries `hole_N_z0` and `hole_N_z1`, and the counterbore is measured from that span. The gate is unchanged: max deviation at most max(4·tol, 0.1), volume error at most 1%.
- y-motor-holder exports both scripts. CadQuery deviates by 0.1843 mm (volume error 0.306%) and OpenSCAD by 0.1843 mm (volume error 0.298%), under the limit of 0.266 mm. On 0.12.0 the same part was refused at 7.924 mm and 30.07% volume: that was the visual hull of the full-length silhouettes. The solid is a base from z=0 to z=8, a taller block and a sloped roof on part of that base, three edge bevels, the 0.5 mm slots, two hexagonal pockets, two shallow bottom pockets, and the screw holes. The Ø3.2 holes along Z run from z=0 to z=8, with an Ø5.6 counterbore 3 mm deep from z=8 and a 0.4 mm 45 degree chamfer at z=0. The base carries a 0.4 mm 45 degree chamfer on the edges that are actually chamfered. STEP is unchanged.
- y-belt-holder stays refused: "combined extrusion deviates by 2.517 mm (limit 0.154), volume error 1.97%". The cross-section changes along X and the belt pocket is a B-spline profile. A stack of planar extrusions does not close that.
- y-rod-holder stays refused: "combined extrusion deviates by 1.503 mm (limit 0.138), volume error 0.31%". The Ø6.3 opening is a partial ring of planes, about 140 degrees, and is not recovered as one cylinder. The body is also not a uniform extrusion: the +Y side reaches y=5.5 only in some bands of z. plug-aligner (0.0000 mm), endstop-block (0.0421 mm), and y-belt-idler (CadQuery 0.1534 mm, OpenSCAD 0.1639 mm) are unchanged.
- Tests: an L-shaped base with a taller block on one arm, and a counterbored hole whose mouth is the step rather than the top of the block, clean and at 0.05 mm noise, for both exporters. Editing the block length and the hole diameter changes the volume by the analytic amount in both scripts.

## 0.12.0 - 2026-10-23
- A cylinder or cone band that meets several planes and coaxial circles in one loop is sewn as a trimmed analytic face. A hole end that is a full chamfer circle can meet a cap split across coplanar pads. A short chamfer glued onto a plane is split back off when its vertices have left that plane. A fillet that only meets planes, including one that runs into a spherical corner, is unchanged and still falls back.
- y-rod-holder is exact: 158 planes, 8 cylinders, 11 cones, Hausdorff 0.2178 mm, volume error 0.391%, 12.4 s. The R=4.00 mm bore and the 45 degree chamfers on it are analytic faces. On 0.11.0 the bore did not sew and the output was the raw mesh (3043 faces, Hausdorff 0, 34.2 s). The 0.22 mm that remains is three shallow patches kept as single planes: vertices 0.16 to 0.24 mm off the plane, and no internal edge sharper than about 10 degrees. Cylinders and cones fit to under 0.005 mm.
- Parametric export of a combined extrusion now accepts a 45 degree axis-aligned chamfer on a hole (`hole_N_chamfer`, the axial length), a blind hole (`hole_N_depth`), a blind pocket, and a shallow floor such as a hex pocket or a logo. When the fused silhouette is more than 3% smaller than a raster of the mesh, the raster outline is used. The gate is unchanged: max deviation at most max(4·tol, 0.1), volume error at most 1%.
- y-belt-idler exports both scripts. CadQuery deviates by 0.1534 mm (volume error 0.144%) and OpenSCAD by 0.1639 mm (volume error 0.145%), under the limit of 0.192 mm. The solid is the X and Y silhouettes, the belt grooves, hex pockets, logo floors, an Ø3.2 hole along X with an Ø5.6 counterbore, a 0.5 mm blind hole along Y with a 45 degree taper, and two Ø3.3 holes along Z with a 0.4 mm 45 degree mouth chamfer. STEP is unchanged: 197 planes, 6 cylinders, 3 cones, Hausdorff 0.0082 mm.
- y-motor-holder stays exact (396 planes, 6 cylinders, 2 cones, Hausdorff 0.1001 mm). Parametric export is refused with "combined extrusion deviates by 7.924 mm (limit 0.266), volume error 30.07%". The Ø5.6 / Ø3.2 screw holes run from z=0.4 to z=8 on a part that spans z=0 to z=19. At a tighter tolerance the same part is refused earlier with "stepped hole is not a counterbored through-hole". The command reports the default-tolerance measurement.
- y-belt-holder stays exact and is still refused, now with "combined extrusion deviates by 2.517 mm (limit 0.154), volume error 1.97%". On 0.11.0 that refusal was 24.461 mm and 90.96% volume. y-rod-holder parametric is refused with "combined extrusion deviates by 1.503 mm (limit 0.138), volume error 0.31%": the Ø3.3 hole along Y is not one radius for the whole length, and a single cylinder sits about 1.5 mm off the mesh where the opening is wider. plug-aligner and endstop-block are unchanged (0.0000 mm and 0.0421 mm).
- Tests: a horizontal bore with a 0.5 mm 45 degree mouth chamfer sews as one cylinder and one cone. A 45 degree chamfer on an X hole through a slanted extrusion is `hole_1_chamfer` in both scripts, and editing it from 0.8 mm to 1.2 mm changes the volume by the analytic ring.

## 0.11.0 - 2026-10-22
- On a clean CAD tessellation, regions that cross a real edge (about 35 degrees) are split and refit. A flat polygon whose corners lie on a circle is no longer stored as a cylinder or a sphere. A short 45 degree chamfer is kept when its vertices lie on the cone. A ring of single-facet planes whose face centres lie on one full cylinder becomes that cylinder. A hole circle split across coplanar cap fragments that do not share an edge is sewn as arcs of one circle. A plane corner whose triple intersection is undefined, or lands far from the mesh vertex, uses that vertex when it lies on the three planes. None of this runs on a noisy mesh, and a fillet between two planes is left alone.
- y-belt-idler is exact: 197 planes, 6 cylinders, 3 cones, Hausdorff 0.0082 mm, volume error 0.003%, 5.1 s. y-belt-holder is exact: 210 planes, 4 cylinders, Hausdorff 0.1602 mm, volume error 0.092%, 6.0 s. y-motor-holder is exact: 396 planes, 6 cylinders, 2 cones, Hausdorff 0.1001 mm, volume error 0.016%, 12.2 s. y-rod-holder is still faceted (3043 faces, Hausdorff 0, 34.2 s): every region is fitted, but the R=4 mm bore meets several chamfers in one loop and does not sew.
- The unfitted area on 0.10.0 was not a torus fillet. Checked against the STEP files at the same commit: planes, cylinders, 45 degree cones, and a few small belt-profile B-splines. The reported gaps were faces glued across a 45 or 90 degree edge, short chamfers, and cylinders left behind after a chamfer was carved out. The idler's eight R=11.1 mm bands, and the motor holder's radii near 8.6, 8.9 and 9.1 mm, were flat faces.
- Parametric export is unchanged for plug-aligner and endstop-block. y-belt-idler, y-motor-holder and y-rod-holder are refused with "a countersink, chamfer or draft on a combined extrusion is not supported yet". y-belt-holder is refused with "combined extrusion deviates by 24.461 mm (limit 0.154), volume error 90.96%". A large outline union no longer calls UnifySameDomain in-process, so that refusal is returned instead of crashing.
- Tests: an octagonal boss on a coarse mesh is 15 planes (not a circumscribed sphere), and a 90 degree countersink about 0.6 mm tall keeps its cone and its cylinder.

## 0.10.0 - 2026-10-21
- Parametric CadQuery and OpenSCAD export now write a small boolean of straight extrusions along X, Y, and Z. Two or three outlines are intersected (`extrude_z_len`, `outline_z`, and the same for X and Y). One outline keeps `extrude_len` and `outline`. A straight cut along another axis is `cut_1_len`, `cut_1_0`, and `cut_1`. A round through-hole stays `hole_N_d`. A counterbored through-hole adds `hole_N_cbore_d` and `hole_N_cbore_depth`, measured from the face the large diameter opens on. Up to four faces tilted to all three axes are planar clips (`clip_1_x`, `clip_1_y`, `clip_1_z`, `clip_1_d`).
- The box script and the single-extrusion script are still written when they already match the mesh, so those parameter names are unchanged. A rectangular plate with a counterbored hole stays `base_x`, `hole_1_d`, `hole_1_cbore_d`, and `hole_1_cbore_depth`.
- Extrusions that are intersected overlap by 0.02 mm in the script so the shared end faces stay a manifold. The other outline stops at the true end, so the solid does not grow.
- Still refused, and the message says which: a boss on an extruded outline; a countersink, chamfer, or draft on an extrusion; a rounded edge that is not the four vertical corners of a rectangular base; a hole that does not pass through; a stepped hole that is not a counterbored through-hole; more than four faces tilted to X, Y, and Z; a recess that is not a straight cut along X, Y, or Z; a sphere; an unfitted region. A flat face that the fitter called a sphere, with most of its vertices on one axis-aligned plane, is read as that plane.
- endstop-block (308 triangles, 13.6×9×14 mm) exports both scripts. The solid is the intersection of the Z, X, and Y silhouettes, an Ø3.3 hole along X with an Ø6.2 counterbore 8 mm deep from the −X end, and one planar clip. The self-check deviation is 0.0421 mm, under the limit of 0.1 mm. plug-aligner is unchanged at 0.0000 mm. y-belt-idler, y-belt-holder, y-motor-holder, and y-rod-holder are still refused: "part has unfitted (faceted) regions".
- Tests: the intersection of two orthogonal extrusions, a block minus a cut extrusion along another axis, and a counterbored through-hole, clean and at 0.05 mm noise, for both exporters. Editing a hole diameter and an extrusion length changes the volume by the analytic amount.

A polygonal through-slot is `cutout_N`. A round through-hole stays `hole_N_d` with the same position parameters as on a box.

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-19
- Clean CAD tessellations are segmented on the raw normals. A mesh whose dihedral angles sit on a few exact values (a CAD export with no noise, including faces that are a single quad) is no longer Taubin-smoothed, and a planar face of one or two triangles is kept. A boundary run of a single mesh edge is kept when the whole loop is coarse; a one-edge flicker between two long runs of the same region is still dropped.
- Two coplanar patches merge only when they share an edge, so disconnected faces on one plane stay separate and the exact builder does not see a non-manifold vertex.
- A sphere fit is rejected when the patch has fewer than eight normal directions. The side walls of a rectangular step lie on a circumsphere; they stay planes.
- On a clean mesh, cylinder strips are chained only across a shallow facet step, and a sphere has to beat that cylinder on the vertices by a real margin. Caps are not absorbed into a cylinder they only touch at the rim.
- `benchmarks/real_parts.py` downloads six Original Prusa i3 MK3S printed parts (Prusa Research, GPL-2.0, not vendored) and prints a fidelity table. CI skips it. `tests/test_coarse.py` converts cadquery solids tessellated once, with no subdivision.
- plug-aligner (48 triangles, 14 planes) is exact, Hausdorff 0, 0.6 s. endstop-block is exact: 15 planes and two cylinders (R 1.65 mm and 3.10 mm), Hausdorff 0.017 mm, volume error 0.20%, 2.0 s. On 0.7.0 both were faceted-hybrid (the aligner had no fitted region; the endstop was 75 planar faces).
- y-belt-idler, y-belt-holder, y-motor-holder and y-rod-holder stay faceted-hybrid or faceted. Each has unfitted patches (edge rounds and belt profiles; 0.5% to 6.6% of the area) and cylinders split by a groove, which is outside the vocabulary, so the exact solid is refused. They finish in 12–39 s. On 0.7.0 the holder, motor holder and rod holder did not finish within 300 s: segmentation was a few seconds, and the time was UnifySameDomain on the faceted fallback after the fit had given up.

## 0.7.0 - 2026-10-19
- Parametric CadQuery and OpenSCAD export now include through-holes, blind holes, and straight cylindrical bosses whose axis is the box X or Y axis, as well as +Z. A side hole is `hole_N_d` plus the two coordinates of its axis (`hole_N_y` and `hole_N_z` for an X hole; `hole_N_x` and `hole_N_z` for a Y hole). A blind hole keeps `hole_N_depth`, measured from the face it opens on. A side boss is `boss_N_d` and `boss_N_h` on that same pattern.
- Countersinks, drafts, and chamfers still have to be vertical. An axis that is not aligned with X, Y, or Z is refused. The script is still executed or rendered and compared with the mesh before it is written (same deviation limits); a mismatch is not written.
- Tests: a 60×40×36 block with an Ø8 through hole along X, an Ø6 blind hole along Y, and an Ø10 boss on the +X face, clean and at 0.05 mm noise, plus a side-hole diameter edit whose volume change matches the analytic cylinder, for both exporters. With no noise the scripts match the mesh to 0.006 mm (CadQuery volume error 0.004%, OpenSCAD 0.003%). At 0.05 mm noise the check against the noisy mesh is 0.139 mm / 0.015% (CadQuery) and 0.149 mm / 0.016% (OpenSCAD), under the existing limit max(4·tol, 0.1) = 0.20 mm. The exact STEP builder does not sew this blind hole (the end disk stays in the cylinder region); STEP falls back to a valid faceted solid, Hausdorff 0.004 mm.

## 0.6.0 - 2026-10-18
- Parametric OpenSCAD export: `mesh2cad convert in.stl --openscad out.scad` writes a Customizer-friendly `.scad` file for the same Z-up parts as the CadQuery script. Parameter names and values match (`base_t`, `hole_1_d`, `hole_1_csk_d`, `boss_1_draft_deg`, `boss_1_chamfer`, `pad_1_w`, `fillet_r`, …).
- Plain CSG: `cube` and `cylinder` (`r1`/`r2` for a draft, chamfer, or countersink), `union` / `difference`, and `offset(r=fillet_r)` for the four vertical corners of a rectangular base. A `// [min:max]` slider is written only where the part has a real bound (hole diameter, fillet, draft, countersink angle, chamfer, pad size). Positions stay plain numbers.
- The script is rendered with the `openscad` CLI and compared to the mesh (sampled Hausdorff and volume) before it is written. The same deviation limits as CadQuery apply. If `openscad` is not installed the file is still written and the command reports it unverified; OpenSCAD is not a Python dependency. Parts outside the vocabulary exit with code 2 and a reason. STEP output is unchanged.
- MCP tool `to_openscad(input_path, output_path?, tol?)`.
- Tests cover each vocabulary element on the synthetic parts (clean and 0.05 mm noise), a hole-diameter edit re-rendered with OpenSCAD, and the unsupported path.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-17
- Constant-radius fillets between two planes are rebuilt as one partial cylinder, tangent to both walls and trimmed by the two end caps. Covers rounded vertical corners of a plate or block, and the inner corner of an L-bracket as well as the outer ones.
- On the synthetic plate (r=5 mm), block (r=2 mm) and L-bracket (r=3 mm) this is exact at 0 and 0.05 mm noise (3/3 seeds). The radius is taken from the cylinder tangent to both walls that fits the arc, so wall triangles left in the patch do not move it. With no noise the radius error is 0.009 / 0.005 / 0.006 mm. At 0.05 mm the worst error is 0.041 mm on the r=2 corners, 0.100 mm on the plate and 0.131 mm on the bracket. At 0.2 mm the bracket falls back on every seed; the plate is exact on 2 of 3 seeds and one of those solids is missing a corner; the block stays valid but one seed keeps only three of the four rounds. The fallback stays a valid solid.
- CadQuery export writes `fillet_r` and `.edges("|Z").fillet(fillet_r)` when the four vertical corners of a rectangular base share one radius, so the radius is a named parameter. An L-bracket fillet stays on the STEP path.
- A fillet that runs into a spherical corner, and a fillet between a plane and a coaxial cylinder (a torus: boss base, rounded hole edge), are not rebuilt.

## 0.4.2 - 2026-10-16
- Narrow chamfers and countersinks that noise breaks into planar strips are gathered back onto the neighbouring cylinder. A chamfered boss stays exact through 0.2 mm noise; a countersink stays exact through 0.05 mm, and on one of three seeds at 0.2 mm.
- When exact sewing fails, the faceted and faceted-hybrid fallbacks are sewn at a tight tolerance and forced onto one parameter range, so the STEP is a valid solid. Coplanar triangles are merged only when that merge stays valid. UnifySameDomain still runs in an isolated process, so a crash keeps the un-merged solid.

## 0.4.1 - 2026-10-16
- Fix: `convert` segfaulted on some parts at the default `--tol` (e.g. a countersunk plate) inside OCCT's UnifySameDomain. Without an explicit `--tol`, tighter fits (0.4×, 0.2×) are now tried before falling back, and the fallback runs face merging in an isolated child process, keeping the un-merged valid solid if OCCT crashes.
- Fix: a fresh `pip install` pulled cadquery-ocp 8.x, whose API breaks the fallback path (`TopoDS.Shell_s`); pinned `cadquery-ocp>=7.8,<8`.
- Regression test for the countersunk plate at default and loose tolerance.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-15

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-14
- Sphere primitive: `Sphere`, `fit_sphere` (normal rays + algebraic fit), `refine_sphere`.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-14
First release.
- Mesh loading/cleaning for STL, OBJ, PLY (trimesh).
- Segmentation into planes and cylinders (region growing, strip-chain cylinder detection, robust cylinder refinement, region merge/absorb).
- Exact B-rep STEP export via OpenCascade for planar parts with through-holes and bosses; hybrid/faceted fallbacks.
